Each item is first scored by rules: base 50 points; freshness (+25 within 6 hours, +20 within 24 hours, +10 within 72 hours); Official sources +10; T3 sources -15; each topic keyword hit (bronx, brooklyn, queens, manhattan, staten island, lottery, affordable, deadline, rental, closing soon, open now) +5, capped at +25; each noise keyword -5, floor -15; total capped at 100.
